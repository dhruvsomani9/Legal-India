# Legal India

A free AI legal assistant that explains legal problems in plain language.

Describe your situation in your own words. Legal India gives you clear, practical guidance based on the Constitution of India and key legal acts.

Built by [Dhruv Somani](https://www.linkedin.com/in/dhruv-somani26), founder of Glow Wellness.

## Why Legal India
Most people face legal problems without knowing their rights or where to start. Legal India makes the first step free, simple, and jargon-free.

## Disclaimer
Legal India provides general legal information, not legal advice. For serious matters, consult a qualified lawyer.
